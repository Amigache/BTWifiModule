BTWifiModule

FrSky Bluetooth emulator for the ESP32-C3 SuperMini.

Requires a Chromium-based browser (Chrome/Edge) over HTTPS or localhost, and the board connected over USB. Press RESET on the board after flashing (the firmware does not auto-reset).

Bluedroid (default)

Standard ESP-IDF Bluetooth host.

NimBLE

Smaller footprint, recommended.

After flashing, connect over serial at 115200 baud and configure the module with the EdgeTX AT commands (see the project README).
